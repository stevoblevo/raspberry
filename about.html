A quiet moment at the well.

A short walk through a courtyard at dusk. A few lines to read, a few deliberate actions to take, and room to go at your own pace.

KK pairs a simple geometric scene with a small, poetic ritual.

Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the round touch pad. Follow the action button as its label changes. With the scene focused, E or Space also performs the action.

Follow the words

Text mode offers the same ritual through descriptions and labeled buttons, without steering through the scene. You can switch modes during your visit.

No account is needed. When browser storage is available, KK saves your place in this browser and offers Continue when you return. You can also begin again. The interactive experience needs JavaScript.
